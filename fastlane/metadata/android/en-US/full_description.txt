Retreat Walk rings a singing bowl at a fixed interval — every 10 minutes, say — over and over until you stop it. For switching partners in a paired exercise, and for sitting with a regular reminder to stay awake.

One number, one button. That is the whole app. Set the minutes between bells and press Start. The bell rings at that many minutes, then at twice, then three times, and so on. Press Stop — in the app or on the notification — and it ends.

Each bell is timed from the start of the session, not from the bell before it, so an hour in the bells have not drifted off the clock.

Why it rings on time:
- Every bell is armed with the exact alarm-clock mechanism the stock Clock app uses, so it fires on time even in Doze with the screen locked.
- The session runs as a foreground service holding a wake lock while the bowl sounds.
- The notification is silent and carries a Stop action, so the session can be ended from the lock screen.
- A session does not survive a reboot: the app shows itself as stopped rather than a countdown that never reaches zero.

The bell is one strike of the Satipanya bowl, loudness-matched to −20 LUFS.

No ads, no tracking, no accounts, no network permission at all.
